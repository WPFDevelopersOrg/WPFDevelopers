# wd:FocusCarousel

> Category: [Layout & Panels](../index.md#layouts-panels) · Available in the WPFDevelopers NuGet package.

Emphasizer carousel (3D flip + zoom effect)

## Example Source

[FocusCarouselExample.xaml](https://github.com/WPFDevelopersOrg/WPFDevelopers/blob/master/src/WPFDevelopers.Samples.Shared/ExampleViews/FocusCarouselExample.xaml)

## Detailed Usage

A carousel with 3D flip + zoom effects, where the center item is enlarged and highlighted:

```xml
<wd:FocusCarousel>
    <Image Source="pack://application:,,,/Images/photo1.jpg" />
    <Image Source="pack://application:,,,/Images/photo2.jpg" />
    <Image Source="pack://application:,,,/Images/photo3.jpg" />
</wd:FocusCarousel>
```
